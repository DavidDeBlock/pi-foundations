I want to explain calmly and clearly what I am working on, because I feel it is not always well understood.

In recent years, I haven't been able to put enough time into the further development of my shop and my systems. Now I really want to make work of that. For me, this feels like a turning point. The goal is to start with a new concept by January 1st: a better website, a reservation module, a parts catalog, our own POS/cash register system, and a much better flow for repairs, customers, orders, and the general operation of the shop.

For me, this is not a hobby project or something I'm just messing around with. This is an investment in my shop, in my way of working, and actually also in our future. If this succeeds, I will be able to work much more efficiently. There will be less chaos, customers will be helped more clearly, everything will come across as more professional, I will have fewer frustrations, and in the long run, also earn more.

But to get that done, I need to be able to put a lot of time and mental space into this project over the coming months. Especially uninterrupted time. I notice that I only really make progress when I am alone, without interruptions, often in the evening or at night. That is not because I want to avoid you or the family, nor is it because I want to shut myself off. It is simply because those are the moments when I get deep enough into the work and can make real progress.

I also realize that the household and the ordinary things must keep happening. I don't want to just push everything away. But I do want to search together for a way in which I get the necessary space during this period, without you feeling like everything falls on you. If there are things we can outsource, make simpler, or distribute differently, I would rather do that than have both of us get frustrated.

What has also been frustrating me lately is not just that I get interrupted or that I lose my focus. It is mostly the feeling that everyone often comes to me for help, while I have almost nowhere to turn to myself. If there is something technical, something practical, something financial, something with choices, something with plans, or something that needs to be figured out, it often ends up with me. And I solve a lot, because I can and because I just figure things out. But in the long run, that feels unbalanced and exhausting.

Sometimes I also need someone who thinks along, figures something out themselves, or takes something over without me having to direct everything. I don't always want to be the one who has to find the solution. That is why I am not only asking for peace and quiet, but also for more self-reliance around me. Not because I don't want to help you, but because I cannot be everyone's helpline all the time.

I don't expect everyone to have as much interest in my project as I do. That isn't necessary. But I would like there to be understanding that this is important to me, to my shop, and to our future. So, I am mainly asking for some space, peace, and understanding until the end of this year.

It is temporarily hard work, with the intention that afterwards, things will become calmer, clearer, and better. I really want to finish this, but for that, I need more uninterrupted time, fewer interruptions, and a bit more trust in the coming months that not everything has to pass through me immediately.
